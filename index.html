Internal Audit — Powered by AI

A traditional internal audit reads a sample. Ours reads all of it, overnight, and again the next night. Findings arrive while the payment can still be stopped and while the person involved still remembers the transaction.

  • Duplicate and near-duplicate payments across entities and banks
  • Entries posted and approved by the same person
  • Vendors sharing a bank account, address or phone number with an employee
  • Round-sum journals, weekend postings, entries backdated into a closed period
  • Purchase orders raised after the invoice date
  • Credit notes issued to customers who never paid
  • Every finding timestamped with the rule it broke and how it was resolved

Tax Management — Powered by AI

Tax problems in a group are almost never discovered by the tax return. They are built into ordinary transactions months earlier. We watch the transactions.

  • Corporate Tax position by entity, tracked through the year rather than at year end
  • Free zone qualifying income monitored, with a warning before mainland revenue puts the status at risk
  • Transfer pricing on every related-party charge, tested against arm's length
  • Transfer pricing disclosure and local file evidence assembled as you go
  • VAT treatment checked by supply: first residential sale, residential lease, commercial, mixed use
  • Input VAT apportionment and reverse charge on imported services
  • Economic substance and ultimate beneficial owner obligations tracked by entity

Security, access and independence

We cannot change your numbers

Access is read-only at the database and API level. There is no route by which Elite Path can post, edit or delete an entry in your ledger. Your audit trail stays yours.

Your data stays in the UAE

Group data is held and processed on UAE-resident infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest, and never used to train anything.

You control who sees what

Every role sees only its own scope. An entity manager cannot see another entity. Salary detail is visible only where you say it is.

Where Elite Path acts as your internal audit function, we do not also prepare the records we are testing. Where we prepare records, an independent reviewer signs the testing. That separation is written into the engagement letter, not left to good intentions.

The first six weeks, practically

No migration project, no parallel run, no training programme. The heaviest thing we ask of you is a read-only login and two conversations.

Days 1–5

Read-only access to each entity's accounting system. Different systems in different companies is normal. Nothing is installed on your machines.

Weeks 2–3

We build one group chart of accounts and map every entity onto it, then identify each intercompany relationship. Around six hours of your CFO's time.

Week 4

The system reads your last complete year and tests it. This first pass is the one that surprises people. You get the findings before any dashboard opens.

Weeks 5–6

Chairman, CEO and CFO dashboards go live. Once those three are trusted, we extend down through operations at your pace.
